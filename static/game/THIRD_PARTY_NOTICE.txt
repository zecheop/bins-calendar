This directory's marble race game (course.js, physics.js, camera.js,
renderer.js, marble-race.js, and parts of ui.js) is an adaptation of:

  lazygyu/roulette
  https://github.com/lazygyu/roulette

used under the MIT License below. Course layouts, physics constants,
camera zoom/tracking behavior, the skill-gauge mechanic, and the impact()
shockwave function were ported from that project and adjusted for this
site (entrance width, skill trigger rate/timing, visual style, etc.).
